Our services

Safety, efficiency and lasting value.

As chimney sweeps in Cologne we actively prevent fires: we inspect flues, measure carbon monoxide and emissions, and advise you on new heating appliances, smoke alarms and saving energy.

Sweeping and inspecting chimneys, heating appliances and flue pipes.

Fireplace inspection

During the fireplace inspection (Feuerstättenschau) all heating appliances in a building are checked for operational and fire safety. We explain the results clearly and tell you what needs to be done.

  • Operational safety
  • Fire safety

Inspections & measurements

Flue inspections, carbon monoxide measurements and emission measurements all focus on the safe operation of your heating appliances. We also carry out gas installation checks according to the German technical rules (TRGI).

  • Flue inspection
  • Carbon monoxide measurement
  • Emission measurement (1. BImSchV)
  • Gas installation check (TRGI)

Cleaning stoves and ventilation systems

Regular cleaning keeps your system safe and efficient. We clean wood stoves, stoves and ventilation systems and, if required, fit chimney caps such as bird guards and ventilation hoods.
