2. Legal basis

  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR – consent (e.g. privacy checkbox in forms, loading Google Maps, email appointment reminder)
  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – contract or pre-contractual measures (e.g. appointment requests and bookings)
  • Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR – legal obligation (e.g. commercial and tax retention obligations)
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interest (e.g. secure operation of the website, answering general enquiries)
  • § 25(1) and (2) TDDDG – storing and accessing information on your device (consent or technical necessity)

5. Contact by phone, email and fax

If you contact us, we process the data you provide (e.g. name, phone number, email address, request) to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your enquiry relates to an order, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries).

Please note: unencrypted emails can in principle be read by third parties in transit. For confidential information we recommend calling us or using the encrypted forms on this website.

6. Contact and appointment request forms

Data processed: name, phone number, email address, message; for appointment requests also the service, property address, preferred date and preferred time of day. Required fields are marked with *.

Purpose and legal basis: handling your request and arranging appointments (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). In addition, we ask for your consent via a checkbox (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without this confirmation we cannot accept the form request – you can still reach us at any time by phone or email. For documentation purposes (Art. 7(1) GDPR), the date and time of consent are noted in the request email.

Transmission: the data is sent to our server encrypted via TLS and forwarded to us by email (email provider: [add provider, data processing agreement]). It is not stored in a database on the web server and not passed on to third parties.

Spam protection: we use a field that is invisible to humans and a time check. No additional personal data is collected and no external services such as reCAPTCHA are used.

Withdrawal: you can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing ritters@schornsteinfeger.koeln. This does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal, nor statutory retention obligations (e.g. for placed orders).

7. Online booking

With online booking you can select an available slot directly. To do so, available time slots are retrieved from our appointment calendar; no data about you is transmitted in this step.

Data processed when booking: selected service, date and time, name, phone number, email address, property address and optional notes. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (carrying out the appointment) and your consent via the privacy checkbox (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Confirmation and reminder: we send the confirmation to the email address provided. You will only receive a reminder the day before if you explicitly select this option (consent, can be withdrawn at any time).

Calendar file: the “Add to calendar” file (.ics) is created directly in your browser; no data is transmitted to us or third parties.

Software used: [add calendar/industry software, provider, server location, data processing agreement]. Until a calendar connection is in place, this function is not offered in live operation.

8. Google Maps

In the contact section you can display an interactive Google Maps map. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”).

The map is only loaded after your consent (privacy notice or clicking “Load map”). Data such as your IP address, date and time, the page visited and browser information is then transmitted to Google; Google may set cookies. Transfers to servers of Google LLC in the USA cannot be ruled out. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which an adequacy decision of the EU Commission exists (Art. 45 GDPR).

The legal basis is your consent (§ 25(1) TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time via the cookie settings. More information: Google privacy policy.

The “Directions with Google Maps” button is a simple link. Google Maps only opens in a new window when you click it; from then on, Google’s privacy terms apply.

9. External links and fonts

Links: our website links to the websites of our partners (Buderus, Viessmann, Vaillant, Brötje) and other providers. No data is transmitted to these providers simply by visiting our website. Only when you click a link do you leave our website; the respective operators are responsible for the linked pages.

Fonts: the fonts “Source Serif 4” and “Nunito Sans” are stored locally on our server. No connection is made to Google Fonts or other font providers.

10. Recipients and transfers to third countries

Your data is only received by us and our processors, who are contractually bound to confidentiality and GDPR compliance: hosting provider, email provider and – once connected – the provider of the calendar software. Data is only transferred to countries outside the EU/EEA in connection with Google Maps and only with your consent (see no. 8). Your data is never sold or used for advertising.

12. Data security

We take technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including: end-to-end TLS encryption (HTTPS, recognisable by the padlock symbol), a strict browser security policy (Content Security Policy), no storage of form data on the web server, regular security updates and backups, and access restricted to authorised persons.

13. Your rights

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to withdraw consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR)

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): where we process data on the basis of legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then no longer process the data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds.

To exercise your rights, an informal message to ritters@schornsteinfeger.koeln or a letter to the address above is sufficient.

14. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia (LDI NRW), Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf, www.ldi.nrw.de.

15. Other information

Obligation to provide data: you are not legally or contractually obliged to provide data via the website. Without the required information, however, we cannot process form requests or online bookings; you can always reach us by phone.

No automated decision-making: there is no automated decision-making including profiling (Art. 22 GDPR). Showing available slots is purely an availability check.

Changes: we update this privacy policy when the website or the legal situation changes. The version published here applies.
